# Admin and staff guide

Sign in, then open **Admin** from the top menu. What you see depends on your role:

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Student | Enrol, track progress, submit projects, earn points, badges and certificates |
| Mentor | Review assigned students' submissions in **Mentor workspace** |
| Content editor | Create and edit courses, lessons and library resources; send them for review |
| Admin | All of the above, plus publish, approve experts and partners, manage inquiries, users and roles |

## Publishing content

Content moves through four states:

1. **Draft:** work in progress, not public.
2. **In review:** ready for a qualified reviewer. Editors use **Send for review**.
3. **Published:** live on the site. Only admins can publish.
4. **Archived:** taken down but kept.

Lesson text uses simple formatting:

- A blank line starts a new paragraph.
- `### ` at the start of a line makes a heading.
- `- ` at the start of a line makes a bullet point.
- `**word**` makes the word bold.
- Checklist items go one per line.

## Library rights

Every resource must record its **licence** before it is saved:

- Owned by JesTech
- Permission granted
- Open licence

Also record the licence details. Do not publish material JesTech does not have the right to share.

Enter the file size in bytes, so visitors see it before they download.

## Experts

New experts are added as **Pending**. Before you approve one:

- Confirm their identity and qualifications or experience.
- Confirm they consent to being listed.

Then tick both boxes and press **Approve**.

## Partners

A partner can only be approved with a **formal agreement reference**. Never list a ministry or institution
without a signed agreement.

## Innovation

- **Challenges** start hidden. **Publish** them when they're ready, and **Close** them when the deadline passes.
- **Case studies** must cite their sources. **Verify & publish** only outcomes that have been confirmed.

## Inquiries and issue reports

Consultation, partnership, contact and solution messages all arrive in **Inquiries**. An email also goes to
`ADMIN_EMAIL`. Update each message's status as you work on it (New → In progress → Closed), and keep internal
notes on it.

Reports of outdated material arrive in **Issue reports**. Correct the content, then close the report.

## Mentors

In **Users and roles**, assign each student to a mentor. Mentors see their students' submissions and either
**Approve** them or **Request changes**, with written feedback.

A certificate is issued automatically when a student has finished every lesson and has an approved project.
